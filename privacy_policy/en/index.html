Privacy Policy

Garmin Connect IQ apps by M. Mendelson

← Back to Apps

Last updated: 14 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all Garmin Connect IQ applications published by M. Mendelson — data fields and watch faces alike, including any apps released in the future (the “Apps”). It explains what usage information the Apps collect, why, and the choices you have.

The Apps are installed from the Garmin Connect IQ Store and run on your Garmin device. Your use of the Garmin platform is also governed by Garmin’s own Privacy Policy, which is separate from this one.

1. Who is responsible

These Apps are developed and maintained by an independent developer, M. Mendelson (the “Developer”, “I”, “me”). For any privacy question or request, contact: mateusmendelson@hotmail.com.

2. Information the Apps collect

To understand how the Apps are used across different devices and to improve them, the Apps send a small usage record when they run. This record can include:

The year of birth and gender are read from the profile you configured in Garmin Connect. If you have not set them, they are not available to the App.

3. You cannot be personally identified

The Apps do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account login, and the information above is not linked to your identity in Garmin Connect or any other account. The device identifier is a value provided by Garmin that only distinguishes one device from another so usage records can be counted and de-duplicated — it is not your name and is not used to work out who you are. Because of this, the information the Apps collect cannot, on its own, identify you as a specific, named person.

4. How the information is used

  • To understand which devices, languages and activity types the Apps are used with.
  • To fix bugs, prioritise improvements, and produce aggregate usage statistics.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

5. Data retention & security

Usage information is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and retained only as long as needed to operate the Apps and produce statistics, after which it is deleted or kept in aggregated form. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect the limited information collected.

6. Children’s privacy

The Apps are general-purpose fitness tools and are not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by local law. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact me and I will delete it.

7. Your rights & choices

  • The age and gender data come from your Garmin profile; you can edit or clear those fields in Garmin Connect at any time.
  • You can uninstall an App at any time to stop it from running and sending any usage information.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the information associated with your device. To do so, email me at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
