This single Privacy Policy applies to all Garmin Connect IQ applications published by M. Mendelson — data fields and watch faces alike, including any apps released in the future — as well as to this website, apps.mmendelson.com (together, the “Apps”). It explains what information the Apps collect, why, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The Apps are installed from the Garmin Connect IQ Store and run on your Garmin device. Your use of the Garmin platform is also governed by Garmin’s own Privacy Policy, which is separate from this one.

1. Who is responsible

These Apps are developed and maintained by an independent developer, M. Mendelson (the “Developer”, “I”, “me”). For any privacy question or request, contact: mateusmendelson@hotmail.com.

2.2 Activity & fitness data

Because these are training tools, the data fields read live activity information from your device — such as heart rate, pace, speed, distance, steps and elapsed time — to calculate and display metrics on your watch. For ordinary data fields and watch faces, this information is processed on your device to show it to you and is not transmitted off the device. The two exceptions are described below.

2.3 Live Tracker

If you use the Live Tracker data field, it streams your ongoing activity to my server so that the activity can be viewed on a live web page. This transmitted data can include: elapsed time, average pace and speed, distance, average heart rate, step count, activity status, sport type, device model, time of day, and a track identifier used to build the shareable tracking link. Anyone who has that tracking link can view the activity while it is live, so only share it with people you trust.

2.4 Route Silhouette watch face

The Route Silhouette watch face pairs your watch with my backend using the device identifier described above, then retrieves a summary of your most recent activity (its name, distance, duration and sport type) and a rendered image of that activity’s route (derived from GPS location data) to display on the watch face.

2.5 Website

The website apps.mmendelson.com uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects standard analytics information (such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type) to measure traffic. This applies to the website only, not to the on-watch Apps.

3. How the information is used

  • To operate and provide the Apps and their features (including live tracking and route display).
  • To understand which devices, languages and activity types the Apps are used with, so I can fix bugs and prioritise improvements.
  • To produce aggregate usage statistics.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

4. Where your data goes (service providers)

To provide the features above, some data is processed by third parties acting on my behalf or as independent platforms:

  • Google — the usage records in section 2.1 are received and stored using Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, and the website analytics in section 2.5 use Google Analytics. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • My own backend (api.mmendelson.com) — receives the Live Tracker and Route Silhouette data described in sections 2.3 and 2.4.
  • Garmin — the Apps run on the Garmin Connect IQ platform and read data from it. Garmin’s handling of your data is governed by Garmin’s Privacy Policy.

Paid features and in-app purchases, where offered, are processed by third-party providers that operate under their own privacy policies; I do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Data retention

Usage and analytics records are retained only as long as needed to operate the Apps and produce statistics, and are then deleted or kept in aggregated form. Live Tracker data is transient and used to display an activity while it is in progress. You can ask me to delete data associated with your device identifier at any time (see section 9).

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect the limited information collected.

7. Children’s privacy

The Apps are general-purpose fitness tools and are not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by local law. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact me and I will delete it.

8. International users, legal bases & your rights

The Apps may be used worldwide, and data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, the legal bases for processing are my legitimate interest in operating and improving the Apps and, where you enable an optional feature (such as Live Tracker or the Route Silhouette pairing), your choice to use that feature.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to processing, or to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact me at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Your choices

  • The age and gender data come from your Garmin profile; you can edit or clear those fields in Garmin Connect.
  • You can uninstall an App at any time to stop it from running and sending data.
  • Only share Live Tracker links with people you want to have access.
  • You can disable cookies for the website through your browser or opt out of Google Analytics using Google’s tools.
  • You can request deletion of data linked to your device by emailing me.

10. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
